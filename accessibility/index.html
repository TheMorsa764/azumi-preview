Statement

Accessibility

Last reviewed [October 2026]. This statement will be reviewed at least once a year and whenever the website changes significantly.

I want everyone to be able to read, explore and get in touch through this website, whatever device, browser or assistive technology they use.

Standard and status

This website aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. It is partially conformant: most of the site meets the standard, and the known exceptions are listed below.

What this website does

You can navigate it fully with a keyboard, using a skip link to the main content and visible focus outlines. It works with screen readers such as VoiceOver and NVDA, and Japanese words are marked so they are pronounced correctly. Pages adapt to any screen size and to zoom up to 400% without sideways scrolling. Animation respects your device’s reduced-motion setting. Under Display settings you can enlarge text, reduce motion, increase contrast, underline links and switch to an easier-to-read font.

Known limitations

Calligraphy artworks are described by their title, meaning and medium; the full visual character of brushwork cannot be conveyed in text, and I am happy to describe a work in more detail on request. The contact and newsletter forms are processed by Formspree and MailerLite, and messages after sending come from those services. Links to Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn and publishers lead to sites I do not control.

How this was checked

Self-assessment in [October 2026] using automated testing (axe-core) and manual testing with a keyboard, a screen reader and at mobile, tablet and desktop sizes.

Feedback and contact

If something does not work for you, or you need information in another format, please write to office@azumiuchitani.com. I aim to reply within [5 working days].
