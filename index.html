Japanese Wisdom

Ancient philosophy for the way we live today.

If you are seeking clarity, balance and a more meaningful way to live, Azumi’s work offers the timeless guidance of Japanese wisdom. She explores the philosophies and practices she grew up with — the Way of Tea, calligraphy, Shu Ha Ri, Kintsugi and Yoshuku — and brings them into everyday contemporary life.

About私

Writing, teaching and art as different paths toward the same understanding.

Born into a traditional spiritual Japanese family, Azumi grew up with tea ceremony, flower arrangement and calligraphy as part of daily life. She built a career in Europe bridging Japan and Europe as a business management consultant, then returned to the practices of her childhood — and made sharing them her work. Today she writes, paints, teaches and speaks internationally, from a living room to a board room.
